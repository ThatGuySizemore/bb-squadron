# Declaring a wait

When you hold back for another lane, such as one that holds the same files, run `bb squadron wait '<SPEC-ID>' --on '<lane or reason>'` once, using this lane's spec identifier and the other lane's identifier where there is one. Then save your work and end your turn.

The board shows "Waiting on <lane>" and keeps the lane out of Needs you, so the Helper isn't woken. The wait clears when you run again or when the named lane lands. It grants no owner approval. A real question for the owner still goes at the start of its own line as `Needs you:`.
